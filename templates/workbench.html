🧠 Theoretical Foundations: Lifecycle & Learning Paradigms

1. Machine Learning Paradigms

Supervised: Learns $f(X) \to y$ from labeled taxi meters to predict fare amounts.
Unsupervised: Discovers latent cluster patterns from density and geometry without labels.
Reinforcement: Learns dynamic fleet dispatch actions through environmental rewards.

2. Training-Serving Skew

Performance decay caused by discrepancies between offline training and live production inference. Prevented in RideRush by wrapping scalers inside Scikit-Learn Pipelines fitted solely on training splits.

3. Feature Store Architecture

Dual storage system: Offline Store (Parquet/Dask) for high-throughput batch training with point-in-time correctness; Online Store (Redis/Key-Value) for sub-10ms REST inference.
